Roadmap to the platinum

Three stages, about 200 hours in total. Keep a manual save at the start of every chapter — there is no mission replay and no New Game Plus — and never use cheats on a trophy run: they disable trophies and saving.

  1. Stage 1 — Story, 5 chapters 60–80 hours

    Play the five chapters and take optional missions as they appear. Scan new vehicles with WAiNK and study animals through the binoculars as you go, so the 100% checklist is shorter later.

    ⚠ The one trophy that may be missable lives here: Positive Mental Attitude. In Chapter 1, after the mission Down to Earth, talk to Lucia in the kitchen at Jason’s house in the morning, follow her to the balcony and accept her invitation to meditate. It is gone after Chapter 1.

  2. Stage 2 — 100% completion checklist ~100 hours

    After the credits the checklist opens under Progress, then Stats, then 100% Completion. Everything you did during the story already counts, and nothing on it is missable. It is lenient — closer to 60% true completion: 70 of 80 challenges, 300 of 397 stunts, 15 of 18 card sets, 200 of 277 vehicles, 100 of 255 animals, 50 of 66 weapons. Only robberies, underwater discoveries, all 42 shooting-range golds, all 42 drone time trials and all 15 of Bret’s Target Decks need everything.

  3. Stage 3 — Cleanup ~10 hours

    Finish the 8 Strangers & Freaks strands and the character questlines (Andy, Kenton, Billy, Randy, Tyrone), then mop up the remaining activity, robbery and money trophies in free roam. The platinum pops when the last of the other 54 is earned.

⚠ Missable: Positive Mental Attitude (bronze) — meditate at every spot across Leonida. The first meditation, Lucia on the balcony at Jason’s house in Chapter 1, disappears after that chapter. Do it, even though it is unconfirmed whether the trophy needs it — it is the safe choice.

About this guide

Built as an offline web app: open index.html from any static server and it works with the network off. Progress is stored in your browser’s local storage and never leaves your machine; export writes a file you can keep or move to another browser, import reads it back. The look is our own — no Rockstar logo, no official artwork, no game screenshots. Trophy names and descriptions are shown as PlayStation publishes them; everything else is written for this guide.
